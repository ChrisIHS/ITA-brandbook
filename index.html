Colour tokens

navy-900#021D39

ITA Navy. The 'I' and IMPACT wordmark colour; the dominant ground on thumbnails, title cards, end screens and banners. White, Sky and Orange all pass contrast on it.

navy-700#0B2E52

Raised navy. Placeholder boxes on end screens, panels sitting on navy-900.

orange-500#EE5D1A

ITA Orange (the 'T'). Headline second line, the diagonal slab, episode numbers. On navy it passes for all text sizes (4.7:1). On white use only at 24px+ (3.6:1); for smaller orange text on white use orange-600.

orange-600#C4470D

Accessible orange for small text and links on white grounds (5.2:1).

sky-300#86B7D7

ITA Sky (the 'A' and TECHNICAL ADVISORS). Eyebrow labels, tags, the case-study slab. Navy text on sky passes (7.9:1); white on sky does not, never use it.

sky-50#EAF2F8

Ice. Light-theme raised surfaces in decks and documents.

white#FFFFFF

Headline first line on navy; the logo pill and question pill fill.

surface#FFFFFF

Page / slide ground.

ink-muted#46607C

Secondary text, captions, eyebrows on surface.

rule#D3E2EE

Hairlines and dividers.
